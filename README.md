# Baltic Awakening

A flavor and content mod for Victoria II focused on Estonia, from the 1836 start through the game's end. Play Estonia as a subject of the Russian Empire and lead it through the national awakening to independence - or release it and play it directly at any point in the campaign. Everything is built on vanilla Heart of Darkness engine syntax and has been audited for stability against a clean game installation.

Content includes a historically accurate literacy start and dozens of decisions and events spanning the whole era: the national awakening (the Kalevipoeg, the song festivals, the Students' Society, the Land Reform), the Soomusrong armored train and its own unit type, the young republic (the Kroon and Eesti Pank, the Kaitseliit and the Narva Line, the Treaty of Tartu), the atheist-secularization chain, the Vaps movement and the December Rising, and a rising Baltic Territorial Army (BLW) with its own flags. Estonia can also form the United Baltic Duchy through vanilla's cultural-union mechanic, and every decision and event applies to it as well.

To install, copy the `Baltic_Awakening` folder and the `Baltic_Awakening.mod` file into your Victoria II installation's `mod` folder, then activate the mod in the launcher. See the [releases](https://github.com/calebjamesrussell/Victoria-II-Baltic-Awakening/releases) page for downloadable versions.
